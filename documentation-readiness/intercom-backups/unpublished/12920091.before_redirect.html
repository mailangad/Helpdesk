View the history, trends and charts of the values entered for a KPI.

Open the KPI

1. On the home screen, click the KPI button to open the KPI dashboard.

2. Click the KPI you want to view. A window opens with the KPI's details.

View the data

3. Scroll down to Recent Trends and click the expand button next to View All.

A window opens with the details and data entered for the KPI.

4. Select the date range, type of data, type of chart and other options.

Good to know

  • View history by week or month.

  • Switch the chart from Bar to Pareto + Trend or Pie. For KPIs with Pareto categories, uncategorized values appear separately.

  • The KPI view also shows linked Kaizens and Recent Trends over one year, and you can export the data to Excel.

  • To show KPIs live in huddles, add them to a Lean Studio dashboard. Heatmap charts are available for KPI data.
