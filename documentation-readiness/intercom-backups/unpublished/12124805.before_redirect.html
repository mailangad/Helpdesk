The Skill Matrix Dashboard (Team Dashboard) is the central place that lists all the teams in a facility. In the app, it is labelled All Teams.

When you create a team, it is saved to the database. It is then available for viewing, analysis and exporting statistics. A company using LeanSuite may have many teams.

To open it, click Skill Matrix on your account home page, then All Teams in the left menu.

This is what a typical Skill Matrix Dashboard looks like:

What the Skill Matrix Dashboard does

  • Shows the number of teams in the facility in four tiles: Total Teams, Pillar Teams, Expansion Teams and Other Teams.

  • Lists all the teams in the facility.

  • Helps you find a particular team.

  • Filters the teams by specific criteria. For example, you can filter teams by the Team Lead's employee classification.

Toolbar and columns

The toolbar has a Search by Id and title box, Filters, Group By, a column picker and a refresh button.

The table shows the columns Team Id, Team Title, Team Type, Pillar, Team Lead and Action.

Dashboard display options

Use the display option in the top-right corner of the dashboard to change how teams are shown.

View (Table or List): the default view is Table, with column headings. Switch to List to show the teams as cards.
