The Reports section of the Kaizen module analyzes the information in submitted Kaizens, such as state, approach, pillar and cost, and turns it into charts.

Watch a video walkthrough (1:20)

Reports show statistics that help your facility understand how Kaizens are created, progressed and closed. Charts give you quick insight into how your facility is working.

Open Kaizen reports

Open Kaizen Reports in the Kaizen menu. The page has five tabs:

  • Overview

  • Benefit & Cost

  • Workload

  • Wall of Fame

  • Delay Management

Use Filters and Saved Views at the top right to narrow the data and reuse your filter settings.

Overview

This tab gives an overview of the whole Kaizen module. It includes:

  • Kaizen by state

  • Kaizen By Approach

  • Kaizen Impact

  • Number of Kaizens By Pillar

Benefit & Cost

This tab goes into more detail. It shows comparative numbers based on the information in your Kaizens. It includes:

  • Average Days to Close

  • Total Benefit

  • Total Cost

  • Total Savings

  • Detailed Savings Data

  • Benefits Vs Cost Comparison

  • Target Vs Actual Benefit

  • Target Vs Actual Cost

Workload

The Workload tab helps you see how project work is spread across your team.

Wall of Fame and Delay Management

The report page also has Wall of Fame and Delay Management tabs.

Good to know

  • Why report totals can differ from the Kaizen Dashboard: reports apply a default year-to-date Created on filter, while the dashboard list follows the facility switcher. Adjust the report filter to compare like with like.

  • For offline analysis of project timelines, use the Delay Management Excel export.

  • Savings only count in results when their savings status is Active. You can set savings info to Active or Inactive in the Result section of a Kaizen.
