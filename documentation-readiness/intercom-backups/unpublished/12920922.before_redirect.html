Preview a template to see its questions before you start, assign or clone it.

Preview a template

1. In the Daily Standard Work menu, go to My Templates.

2. Click the Preview button next to the template you want to preview.

3. Review the questions in the side window that opens. This gives you a quick overview before starting an activity.

4. Click the close (X) icon to close the window and preview another template.

Good to know

  • Preview is also available in the Marketplace, so you can check a template before cloning it.

  • When you build a template in Lean Creator, the right-hand pane shows a live phone preview as you add questions.
