You can open a Kaizen from the Kaizen Dashboard in two ways: the side window or the Kaizen detail view.

Watch a video walkthrough (1:19)

  • Side window: shows a summary of the Kaizen in a smaller, scrollable panel while the Kaizen dashboard stays open. You can pick any Kaizen and see an overview of it at the same time.

  • Kaizen detail view: opens the Kaizen in a full-page view where you can see its different sections at once. It shows more information than the side window, and the dashboard is not visible.

Open the side window

1. Go to the Kaizen Dashboard. To learn more about it, see About the Kaizen Dashboard.

2. Click the Kaizen you want to open. The side window opens with the Kaizen's information.

Tip: click the Copy icon in the top-left corner of the side window to copy the Kaizen's ID to your clipboard.

Open the Kaizen detail view

3. In the side window, click ID: Kaizen ID in the top-left corner.

Note: the side window and the Kaizen detail view show the same information in different ways.

Good to know

The Kaizen dashboard also has a Gantt (timeline) view, a Revised Due Date column, delay tracking (days delayed) and saved views with filters.
