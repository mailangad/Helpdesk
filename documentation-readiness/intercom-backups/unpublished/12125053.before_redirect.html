Add a comment to an F-Tag to discuss it with others while it is implemented. Comments are in the F-Tag's Activity section.

Watch a video walkthrough (1:19)

Steps

1. Open the F-Tag you want to comment on, in the side window or the detail view.

Need help? See Open an F-Tag after creation.

2. Go to the Activity section of the F-Tag.

3. In the Show options, choose Comment to display the comment section.

4. Type your comment in the Add a Comment box and click the arrow at the far right of the box.

Result

Your comment appears in the list below the comment box.
