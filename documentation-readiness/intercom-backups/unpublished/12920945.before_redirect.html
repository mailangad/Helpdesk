Copy a ready-made template from the Marketplace into your own workspace so you can adapt and assign it.

Clone a template from Daily Standard Work

1. In the Daily Standard Work menu, go to My Templates.

2. Click the Marketplace tab, next to My Templates.

3. Find the template you want. Click Preview first if you want to look inside.

4. Click Use template on its card.

Result

The cloned template appears in My Templates. You can now use it, preview it, edit it (for example to add rules) and assign it.

Clone a template from Lean Creator

The Marketplace is also available in the Lean Creator navigation. If you don't see it, refresh the page.

  1. In Lean Creator, click Marketplace. It opens the Marketplace tab shown above.

  2. Click Preview to look at a template.

  3. Click Use template to clone it into My Templates.

Available templates

Available templates include:

  • 5S

  • Gemba Walk and Safety Gemba Walk

  • Kamishibai

  • Forklift Inspection

  • Industrial Safety

  • Safety Observation

  • Leadership HSE

  • Risk Assessment

  • LOTO

  • Video SOPs with comprehension questions

Note: Your own templates stay private unless you save them with Public visibility, which publishes them to the Marketplace for other companies.
