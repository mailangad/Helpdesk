When a Suggestion is created, it collects information through various fields as per requirement.

This information is stored in all Suggestions, available to see through the dashboard.

After a Suggestion is submitted into the database, the information entered can be edited according to need as a Suggestion proceeds further.

There are two scope in which the information can be edited:

Properties Information

Information entered while creating a new Suggestion.

This scope covers the integral information that forms a Suggestion, ex. problem, recommendation, pillar, department, labels etc.
