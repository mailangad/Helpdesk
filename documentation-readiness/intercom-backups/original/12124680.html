Step 2

Navigate to the Team Dashboard by clicking on the Skill Matrix button :Skill_Matrix_System: on the home page.

Step 3

Choose and click on the team you wish to see the data for a particular date.

The Team Detail window will open up.

Step 4

Navigate to the right corner under the Select Date Box and click on Edit Team.

Step 5

The Edit Team window will open up.

Enter the new information in the fields as required and follow the same steps as done while creating a new team!
