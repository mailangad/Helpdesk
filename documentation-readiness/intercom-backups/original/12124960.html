Step 2

There are 2 ways to open a Best Practice:

  • Side Window: A Side window displays a summarized view of the Best Practice information in a smaller scrollable area with the Best Practice dashboard still open. This lets you choose any Best Practice and get an overview of the information inside it simultaneously.

  • Best Practice Detail View: This view opens up the Best Practice in a full-page view with different sections available to see at once. This displays more information as compared to the side window and there is no dashboard visible in this view.

Side Window

To open the side window select & click the Best Practice you wish to open. A side window will pop up with the Best Practice information inside it.

Useful Tips!

The :Copy_Icon_Transparent: Icon present on the top left of the side window copies the ID of the Best Practice on your clipboard!

Best Practice Detail View

To open the Best Practice detail view, click on the “ID: Best Practice ID” located on the top left corner of the side window.

Useful Facts!

The Side window & Best Practice Detailed view display the same information in different ways!
