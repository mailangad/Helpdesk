People form the most important part in a facility.

Various employees working together inside a facility with different skills, expertise form a strong team that can handle the production, safety, improvement and all other crucial aspects.

This team aspect; people of similar and different expertise coming together for various tasks in a workspace form the core of Skill Matrix inside Lean Suite.

Theskill matrix is the central place where teams can be created, managed, analyzed and improved by setting targets!

Along with team management options, individual performance, gap closures, tool competencies can also be viewed, recorded, analyzed and improved through the various tools and features inside Skill Matrix!
