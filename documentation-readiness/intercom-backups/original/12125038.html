The Tag Settings window is scrollable. The Priority & Trade Type windows are available side by side right below the Default Due Date/Issues window. You can reach it simply by scrolling down in the Tag Settings window.

The issues that are raised inside F-Tag are assigned to a particular person/employee inside the facility.

The assignment is done keeping in mind the expertise of the assignee to make sure the issue is resolved properly. These expertise are based on the Trade Skills of the person concerned.

An equipment repair can be caried out be an engineer therefore the trade would correspond to engineering. Various trades can be set up in the F-Tag configuration settings and they can be attached to the Trades field while creating a new F-Tag!

Adding a trade type and other settings that we learned before add to a whole process of adding additional information and detailing out the F-Tag. This becomes an important part in the functioning information of a facility where administration can minutely analyze the data and look at which issues have been reported, how, when and by whom (trade types represent the whom in many scenarios) they were resolved, the delays and resources used etc.

All this information becomes instrumental in the management and improvement of a facility’s day to day operations!

The Window lists all priorities in a list manner with their default due days in front of the name.

Tips & Tricks

Use the drop-down option on the bottom left to control how many priorities are visible at a time.
