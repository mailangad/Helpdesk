Suggestion system inside Lean Suite provides a central platform where employees/users at all levels according to their permissions and authority can provide an insight or input into a process, workflow, system, practices etc. This insight, handled as a suggestion in Lean Suite is reviewed by concerned people and taken action upon.

The suggestion system aims to provide a comprehensive tool where small and big suggestions about all aspects inside a facility can be gathered, reviewed and implemented to bring about positive changes, modifications and outputs inside a facility.

The Suggestion system also houses a Suggestion Campaign space where suggestions can be welcomed for a particular aspect bounded by a time frame.

All the suggestions submitted carry a reward in terms of points that can be accumulated and further exchanged for prizes.

Overall, the suggestion system creates a holistic work environment where improvement ideas are welcomed as suggestions and fruitful initiatives are rewarded.
