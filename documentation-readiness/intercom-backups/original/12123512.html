Step 1

Navigate to the Tool Management Dashboard.

Step 2

Select the tool you wish to edit and click on the three dots icon on the extreme right under the Action column.

Two nested options namely Edit & Delete will show up.

Step 3

If you wish to delete a tool, the delete option can be selected if not click on the Edit option to proceed with the editing process.

Step 4

The Update Tool window will show up with the same information fields that were available while tool creation.

Change or edit the information in the fields as per requirement and click on Update button on the top right corner of the window.

Step 5

Once you click on submit. A green colored pop up confirm the changes made to the Tool.

Congratulations! We have successfully learned how to update a tool in Kaizen!
