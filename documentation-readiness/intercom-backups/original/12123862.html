Need Help?

https://app.intercom.com/a/apps/lhkbxbyv/knowledge-hub/internal-article/4673048

Step 3

Choose the Loss Tree you wish to delete. Navigate to the right end of the row and click on the three dots icon under the Action Column.

Step 4

Choose the Delete option to remove the Loss Tree from the database.

A browser pop-up will confirm your decision. Click Ok ro proceed.

Congratulations! We have successfully learned how to delete a Loss Tree!
