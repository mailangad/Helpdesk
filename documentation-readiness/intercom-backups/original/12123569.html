Step 3

Choose and click on the Phase you wish to add a section to (phase 2 chosen in example picture; no phases have been added, therefore no options are visible under the phases tab).

Step 4

Once the phase is selected, it will be highlighted in Blue.

Adding a Section

Choose which section you wish to add to the selected Phase.

In case you do not see the section in the list below Phase Tab, click on & More drop down button to view all available sections that can be added.

When a section is selected, it will appear below the sections list tab and a green colored pop upwill confirm the addition!

You can choose various sections from the list and the new ones will appear in the list below as newly added sections!

Removing a Section

Choose which section you wish to remove from the list of sections added below.

Click on the cross icon in front of the section to remove it.

A browser pop up will confirm your decision. Click Ok to proceed further with deletion.

Useful Facts!

While adding a section if you cannot find a section you wish to add, you can create a new section ad add it to the concerned phase!
