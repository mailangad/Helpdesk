Step 1

Log into your Lean Suite account with your credentials.

Need help? https://app.intercom.com/a/apps/lhkbxbyv/knowledge-hub/internal-article/4672769

Step 2

Navigate to the top right corner of the window and click on Create New.

Step 3

Select “Suggestion” from the drop-down menu.

Step 4

A new suggestion window will show up with various fields to collect information about the new suggestion.

Enter the required information as required in the fields.

Tip: Use the new Lean AI for rewriting the problem as well as suggestions !

Simply click on the star icon located in the top right corner of dialogue box and the Lean AI window will open up providing various options for rewriting the text.

Lean AI Window

Step 5

After entering all the information, click on Submit on the top right corner to finish the process of creating a suggestion.

Tip: The cancel button on the side cancels the process and takes you to the home page.

Congratulations, we have successfully learned how to create a suggestion!

Now that we know how to create a suggestion, let’s dive into some details while creating suggestions!

Notice the “Show Fields” alongside the Submit and Cancel Button? This option helps you add or edit the information fields in the creation process.
