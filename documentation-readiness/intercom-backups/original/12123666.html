Step 2

There are 2 ways to open a Kaizen:

  • Side Window: A Side window displays a summarized view of the Kaizen information in a smaller scrollable area with the kaizen dashboard still open. This lets you choose any kaizen and get an overview of the information inside it simultaneously.

  • Kaizen Detail View: This view opens up the kaizen in a full-page view with different sections available to see at once. This displays more information as compared to the side window and there is no dashboard visible in this view.

Side Window

To open the side window select & click the Kaizen you wish to open. A side window will pop up with the kaizen information inside it.

Useful Tips!

The :Copy_Icon_Transparent: Icon present on the top left of the side window copies the ID of the kaizen on your clipboard!

Kaizen Detail View

To open the Kaizen detail view click on the “ID : Kaizen ID” located on the top left corner of the side window.

Useful Facts!

The Side window & Kaizen Detailed view display the same information in different ways!
