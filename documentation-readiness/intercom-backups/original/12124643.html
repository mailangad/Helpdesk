Step 2

Navigate to the Team Dashboard by clicking on the Skil Matrix button :Skill_Matrix_System: on the home page.

Step 3

Choose and click on the team you wish to clone.

The Team Detail window will open up.

Step 4

Click on the Clone icon right next to the Team Name.

Step 5

A new Create Team window will open up.

Since the team is being cloned, the information fields already have the same information pulled from the source team.

Enter the new information in the fields if required and follow the same steps as done while creating a new team!
