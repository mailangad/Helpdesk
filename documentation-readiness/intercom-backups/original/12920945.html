Step 1

Navigate to the My Templates option from the parent menu of the Daily Standard Work system.

Step 2

Next, locate and click on the Marketplace located next to the My templates option.

Use the Clone Template button on the right most corner to clone the template of your choice.

Step 3

Once you have cloned a template, it will show up in the My Templates section.

From there on it is available to use, preview or assign to a user using the Daily Standard Work system!

Congratulations ! We have successfully learned how to clone a template and from the maretpplace!
