Step 1

Log into your Lean Suite account with your credentials.

Step 2

Once you sign into your account, click on the Create New tab on the right side of the main menu bar.

Step 3

From the drop-down menu select Kaizen.

Step 4

The Create Kaizen window will open up on your page.

Enter the information in the fields provided.

A create kaizen window collects information through various fields that can be added/edited as per need of the process in question.

Scroll to the bottom to learn more about fields.

Step 5

Once you have added all the information, click on Submit on the top right corner.

A green colored pop up will show on the top right corner confirming the submission of your new kaizen.

Useful Tips!

Once a kaizen is created, it automatically opens up in a detail view to make further changes or proceeding further.

Congratulations, you have successfully created a new Kaizen. 😀
