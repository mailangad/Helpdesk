Step 3

Scroll down to the Quiz Comparison field.

Click on the Compare button on the top right corner of the field.

Step 4

The graph will be divided into two equal parts for the quizzes to be compared.

Step 5

Each area will hava two drop-down menu boxes to choose a quiz each for comparison.

Click on the drop-down arrow in both boxes and choose the quizzes to compare.

The comparison data in terms of Correct & Incorrect answers will be visible for each chosen quiz.
