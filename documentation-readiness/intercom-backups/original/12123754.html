About 5G Section

The "5G's" principle in lean manufacturing represents a holistic approach to achieve operational excellence, emphasizing the importance of understanding, respect, and continuous improvement.

By focusing on the 5G's which are Gemba, Gembutsu, Genjitsu, Genri, and Gensoku an organization can effectively identify opportunities for improvement, driving efficiency, and fostering a culture of continuous learning and growth.

Let us understand this with a flowchart:

5G in Lean Manufacturing

5G’s

Descriptions

Purpose

Gemba

Go to the actual place where work is done to observe operations firsthand and identify inefficiencies.

Gain insights into the actual work environment and identify opportunities for improvement.

Gembutsu

Go to the source to see and understand the actual products, components, or materials used in the production process.

Identify root causes of problems and implement effective solutions to improve quality and efficiency.

Genjitsu

Base all decisions and actions on real data, facts, and observations rather than making assumptions.

Gain insights into the current state of operations and make informed decisions to drive improvement.

Genri

Understand and apply fundamental lean principles such as respect for people, continuous improvement, and waste reduction.

Create a foundation for operational excellence and continuous improvement.

Gensoku

Establish standardized work practices, guidelines, and rules to ensure consistency, efficiency, and quality in operations.

Foster consistency, efficiency, and a culture of continuous improvement within the organization.
