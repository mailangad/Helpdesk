Step 4

Choose the course you wish to assign to a user. Navigate to the right end of the course row and click on the three dots under the Action column.

A small window will show up with 3 options.

Choose the Assign Course option.

Step 5

A Side Window will open up with a drop-down box where you can serch the name of the user you wish to assign the course to.

Tips & Tricks

You can assign a course to more than one person at a time

Just search for the names and click on the result. A list of the assignees will be created below!

To remove an assignee simple type backspace in the search box!

Once you have added all the required assignees, click on Assign button on the bottom right!

A green colored pop-up notification will confirm the assignment of the course!
