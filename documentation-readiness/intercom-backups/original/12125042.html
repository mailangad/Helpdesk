Step 2

Click on the Export Tags on the top right corner of the dashboard.

Useful Tips!

If you click on the drop-down arrow on the right it would display one more option i.e Export Assignee.

This export options extracts the F-Tag assignee for the searched tags then collects the statistics of the assignee. This assignee data is available to export in this option.

Step 3

Choose your preferred location on the system you are using and enter the name of the file you want to export.

Click on Save when you are done.

Congratulations! You have successfully exported your search results in a from the F-Tag dashboard!
