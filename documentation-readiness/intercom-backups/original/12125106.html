This manual will focus on the second part of the course creation i.e. Course Creation.

Once a course is created it is added to the database and becomes visible in the Instructor Panel as well as the All Courses dashboard.

As an instructor you can navigate to any of the above and choose the course you wish to create!

Step 1

Navigate to the Instructor Panel or All Courses Dashboard and find the course you wish to create.

Step 2

Click on the course and it will open up the Course Creation window.

Step 3

The Course Creation window has 5 phases through which an instructor can enter information and add properties, pre-requisites, evaluations etc. to a course.

Enter the information in all the phases and when you are finished click on Publish on the top right corner to make the course available in the workspace.

Smart Connections

Curating a course can be a long and complex process depending on how much knowledge a course or training aims to impart in one go.

The Course Creation window allows an instructor to come back and curate the course over time and hence it can save the course as Draft before it gets published. In fact a user can save course data phase wise as well!
