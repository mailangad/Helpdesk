The instructor panel provides a quick way to navigate directly to the analytical data of a particular course!

Step 1

Log into your Lean Suite Account with your credentials.

Need Help ?

https://app.intercom.com/a/apps/lhkbxbyv/knowledge-hub/internal-article/4672769

Step 2

Navigate to the Instructor Panel in the parent menu inside LMS.

Need Help ?

https://app.intercom.com/a/apps/lhkbxbyv/knowledge-hub/internal-article/4673022

Step 3

Open the All Courses dashboard to view all courses available in the database.

Need Help ?

https://app.intercom.com/a/apps/lhkbxbyv/knowledge-hub/internal-article/4673028

Step 4

Choose the course you wish to assign to a user. Navigate to the right end of the course row and click on the three dots under the Action column.

A small window will show up with 3 options.

Choose the Course Analytics option.

Step 5

A new LMS Reports windowwill show up with course analytics tab open.

The course analytics is the connection between Instructor Panel and the LMS Reports Section.

This is that the Course Analytics Section looks like:

The Course Analytics section shows the analytical data in three main fields:

  • Course Details (Pei Chart)

  • Quiz Benchmarking

  • Quiz Comparison
