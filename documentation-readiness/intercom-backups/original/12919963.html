Step 1

Open the Lean Creator Dashboard by clicking on the Lean Creator Dashboard and button on the home page.

Step 2

Simply click anywhere on the template you wish to edit.

The template will open in the Lean Creator Window allowing you to edit/add/delete the questions and make changes to the responses as per requirement.

Step 3

Once the changes are made, navigate to the top right corner and click on Save to save the template or Save & Publish to directly publish the newly made changes.

A pop-up notification on the top right corner confirms the decision.

Congratulations! We have learned how to edit questions & responses in template!
