Step 1

Step 2

Select Settings from the three provided tabs on top.

Step 3

Navigate to the top right corner above the Default Due Date list.

Click on the arrow on the right and select Default Due Date View.

This will open the Default Due Date Window.

Useful Facts!

The Settings tab in F-Tag helps us set up 4 settings, two of which are set in one place while the other two have their own respective windows.

The Default Due Date & Issues share the same window.

The Priority and Trade Type have their respective windows.

Step 4

Choose the Pillar whose default date needs to be edited.

Navigate to the end of the row and click on the green pencil under the Action column.

A new Update Default Due Dates will open up.

Step 5

Enter the Information as required and click on Save.

The pillar information will be update with the new date entered!

Congratulations! We have successfully learned how to edit the default due date in F-Tag Module!
