Tips & Tricks

The Kaizen Overlay Window serves as the connection point between the Cost Management and Kaizen & Skill Matrix System.

You can open the Kaizen in Detail By clicking on the of the kaizen.

You can copy the Kaizen ID by clicking on the copy icon.

You can also view the Competency of the team associated with the Kaizen buy clicking on the button.
