When a F-Tag is created inside a workspace it carries information inside it which is crucial to its implementation since the aim is to get the issue submitted by the F-Tag resolved as soon as possible.

The F-Tag goes through various stages after creation where it is submitted, verified, implemented or sent back. All these processes are done according to the real-life scenarios related to the F-Tag operation and are depicted in Lean Suite by the Status of a F-Tag.
