Step 1

Navigate to the My Templates option from the parent menu of the Daily Standard Work system.

Step 2

Next, choose and click on the Preview button next to the template you wish to preview.

Step 3

A side overlay window will open up showing the questions inside the template.

This helps quickly take an overview before starting an activity!

Click on the cross icon to close the window and preview another template.

The preview option is also available in the Marketplace section to preview before cloning the template!

Congratulations! We have successfully learned how to preview a template!
