Step 2

Navigate to the top right corner of the window and click on Create New.

Step 3

Select “F-Tag” from the drop-down menu.

Step 4

A new Create F-Tag window will show up with various fields to collect information about the new F-Tag.

Enter the required information as required in the fields.

While you enter the information, a visual representation of the tag updates simultaneously on the right side.

Step 5

After entering all the information, click on Submit on the top right corner to finish the process of creating a F-Tag.

Congratulations, we have successfully learned how to create a F-Tag!
