Useful Facts!

A quiz is not available to enroll or take as a standalone part therefore it is not listed inside the Course Catalog window. A quiz is integrated inside a course as an evaluation criterion.

This manual will focus on the second part of the quiz creation i.e. Quiz Creation.

Once a quiz is created it is added to the database and becomes visible in the Instructor Panel as well as the All Quizzes dashboard.

As an instructor you can navigate to any of the above and choose the quiz you wish to curate!

Step 1

Navigate to the Instructor Panel or All Quizzes Dashboard and find the quiz you wish to create.

Step 2

Click on the quiz and it will open up the Quiz Creation window.

Step 3

The Quiz Creation window has 3 phases through which an instructor can enter information and add questions, properties and necessary information to a quiz.

Enter the information in all the phases and when you are finished click on Publish on the top right corner to make the quiz available in the workspace.

Congratulations! We have successfully learned how to curate a quiz inside LMS!
