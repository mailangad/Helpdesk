The Suggestion Campaign inside suggestion system is a tool designed to facilitate the submission, analysis, and implementation of innovative ideas within the organization through suggestions.

Itis a streamlined tool designed for welcoming and implementing valuable suggestions within the organization. It acts as a centralized hub where employees can submit their ideas as suggestions. Once submitted, the suggestions undergo analysis by concerned authorities.

The outcome of selected ideas is then implemented using the suggestion system’s standard proceedings. The Suggestion Campaign essentially simplifies the journey from idea generation to implementation, encouraging a continuous flow of improvements throughout the company.
