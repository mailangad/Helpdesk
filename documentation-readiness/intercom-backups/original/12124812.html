As an employee/user you also form a part of the workforce of your company and hence a part of the team corresponding to your department or division.

You & Your Team, form the part of various teams and users that are analyzed using the skill matrix. Your achievements, expertise, learnings over time increase your competency and hence allow you to grow inside your workplace.

This personal data of your performance and skills is what User Profile is made of.

The My Profile, in Skill Matrix shows your performance and competency in form of a Radar Chart, tabular form, the teams you are a part of and the history of your tool usage.

There are three main sections inside User Profile:

My Radar Chart

This section showcases:

  1. Competency Radar Chart

  2. Competency Table

  3. All Tools Used by you.

Teams

This section displays the teams you are part of inside the workplace.

History

This section showcases the history of your progress with the tools that have been used by you in the past.
