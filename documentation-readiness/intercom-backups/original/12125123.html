Step 3

Choose and click on the course you wish to open in detail view.

The course can be opened through both table and grid view.

Table View

Click anywhere in course row to open the detail view.

Grid View

Click anywhere on the course card to open the detail view.

Useful Facts!

In the course card, prefer to click in the area except the Add to Wishlist button, the heart shaped icon and the Enroll Now button.

The course will open up with all the details and options to Add to Wishlist or Enroll on the right side.

The Course detail window is scrollable and can be scrolled down to see more details of the course in focus.
