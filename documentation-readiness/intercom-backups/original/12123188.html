Step 1

Log into your Lean Suite Account.

Need Help?

Step 2

Go to the Suggestion Campaign Dashboard by clicking on the Campaigns button in the parent menu on the home page.

Step 2

Navigate to the campaign card you wish to edit and click on the three dots located on the top right corner.

Choose edit or delete as per preference.

Step 3

A new Update Campaign window will open up with the same options/fields as the Create New Campaign Window.

Update Campaign Window

Enter the information as required for update.

Once the fields have been updated with the new information changes click on the Update Campaign button in the bottom.

The campaign will be updated with the new information.

Useful Tips!

The cover image of the campaign image can also be changed by clicking on the Image icon on the top right of the cover image.

Congratulations! You have successfully learned to edit an existing campaign!
