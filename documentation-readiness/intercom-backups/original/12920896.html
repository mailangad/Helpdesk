Once an activity is completed, it is submitted in the form of a report available to view and analyze.

Let's see how to view the report for a submitted activity!

There are two ways to view the report, one, through the overview dashboard and second through the Kanban Board Section.

Overview Dashboard

Navigate to the Overview Dashboard from the home page.

Choose the activity group and the type of activity you wish to see the reports for.

Navigate the Status Window and choose the Submitted button. Now, go to the far right corner and click on View Report.

A new window will open up showing the responses submitted in the activity!

On the right side is the navigation pane that allows you to navigate to the different pages in the report.

The report window has an overview section and pages that are collapsible.

Congratulations! We have successfully learned how to view a report in the DSW System!
