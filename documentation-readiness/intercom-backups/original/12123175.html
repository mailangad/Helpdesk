A “Suggestion” in its core is a notification requiring or asking for attention to things that can be improved, modified or simply taken care of in case there is a working problem involved.

The suggestion can at times require immediate attention based on how big an effect it can have after implementation. Therefore, it is important for the suggestion maker to convey the priority of the suggestion being made.

After the suggestion has been created the user can change the priority of the suggestion to aim towards a faster analysis and if needed implementation.

Priority of a suggestion is a part of the Property Information of a suggestion. It can be edited the same way as any other property information.

A user can follow the steps of editing information in a suggestion, identify the Priority field in the given information and change it to one of the three options available i.e. High, Medium & Low.

Useful Tips!

The priority being property information can be edited in both the side window & detail view!

Now you can analyze how important your suggestion is in nature and follow below to change the priority accordingly!
