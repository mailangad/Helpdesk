Step 1

Log into your Lean Suite Account with your credentials.

Need Help?

Step 2

Navigate to the LMS Module Dashboard by clicking the LMS Button.

Step 3

Click on Course Catalog from the parent menu on the left.

This will open up the Course Catalog Dashboard on the right side.

Step 4

Choose or Search for the course according to your preference. You can use the various search criteria located on the top to filter the course database.

⏬ Few things to know about the search and search results!

The results are displayed in either Grid or Table format. The Grid format displays the courses as Course Cards while the Table View displays the courses in a tabular form.

Grid View

Table View

Tips & Tricks

The Course Card displays information about the course and contains a couple of actions inside it to show the main overview of the course quickly.

Let’s have a closer look at the Course Card below!

The Course card has information and buttons arranged in a visual way:

  • The Image is the cover image of the course.

Smart Connections

The cover image can be chosen during course creation.

https://theleansuite.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/KC/pages/edit-v2/139034660?draftShareId=256734f1-143e-4e65-971a-9438624c6a25

  • The heart button can be used to add the course to the user’s Wishlist.

  • The numerical value next to the heart icon displays the course duration in minutes.

  • The immediate text below the cover image is the title field that has the Course Name & below that the author/instructor who created the course.

  • The three options below the title field,

    display more information about the course such as whether the course is Offline or Online, Public or Private and the Frequency of the course.

  • Lastly the Enroll button that lets a user enroll into the course.

Step 5

Once you have found the course that you wish to enroll into, you can proceed to enrolls in two ways:

Enroll through Dashboard

In Grid View

  1. Click on the Enroll Now button located at the end of the Course Card.

In Table View

  1. Click on the Enroll Now button under the Action column.

Enroll through Course Detail View

Open the course in Detail View.

Need Help?

https://app.intercom.com/a/apps/lhkbxbyv/knowledge-hub/internal-article/4673026

Navigate to the right-side panel of the detail view and locate the Enroll Now button below the Wishlist icon.

Click on the button to enroll in the course.

Congratulations! We have successfully learned how to enroll into a course in LMS!

Now that we have learned how to find a course and enroll into one, let’s move ahead and learn more about the capabilities of the LMS Module!

The LMS system is designed to let a user create a course which other users can enroll into! There are various tools that help create knowledge content, lectures, quizzes etc. to effectively create both small and large courses that can be used to impart necessary training and education to the employees/workers in a workspace!

All of this happens in the Instructor Panel!

So, let’s take up the role of an instructor and see how a course is created, integrated and evaluated inside LMS!
