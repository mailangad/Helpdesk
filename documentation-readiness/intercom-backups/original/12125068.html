Step 2

There are 2 ways to open a F-Tag:

  • Side Window : A Side window displays a summarized view of the F-Tag information in a smaller scrollable area with the F-Tag dashboard still open. This lets you choose any F-Tag and get an overview of the information inside it simultaneously.

  • F-Tag Detail View : This view opens up the F-Tag in a full page view with different sections available to see at once. This displays more information as compared to the side window and there is no dashboard visible in this view.

Side Window

To open the side window select & click the F-Tag you wish to open. A side window will pop-up with the F-Tag information inside it.

F-Tag Detail View

To open the F-Tag detail view click on the located on the top left corner of the side window.

Congratulations! You have successfully learned how to open a F-Tag after creation!
