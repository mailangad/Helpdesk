The Top Losses Report for a loss tree can be viewed in three different ways.

  1. From the Top Losses Section in Reports by selecting the Loss Tree from the drop-down menu.

https://app.intercom.com/a/apps/lhkbxbyv/knowledge-hub/internal-article/4673051

  1. From the Loss Tree Dashboard under the Action Tab. There are two options under the action tab, Top Losses & Delete. Follow the manual below.

https://theleansuite.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/KC/pages/edit-v2/202244118?draftShareId=8b377d28-7af5-4516-ac75-1273e0eacfdf

  1. From the Loss Tree Detail View. The last will be covered in the instruction below!

The Drop-down menu lists all the trees (same as in the Loss Tree Dashboard) available to view details for!
