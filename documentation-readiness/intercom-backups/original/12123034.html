Once a user/employee creates a suggestion, it goes into the database where it becomes available for editing, analysis and proceeding further.

A company using Lean Suite may have hundreds of Suggestions being created by their many employees.

A Suggestion Dashboard is the central place where all the Suggestions created are listed and are available to view, edit, proceed etc.

You can reach the Suggestion dashboard by clicking on the Suggestion button on your account home page.

This is what a typical Suggestion Dashboard looks like:

The Suggestion Dashboard:

  • Lists all created Suggestions.

  • Helps you find your submitted Suggestion.

  • Filters the Suggestion based on specific criteria.

  • Provides a .csv export feature for the database.

The Suggestion dashboard lists the created Suggestions under various headings (20 to be exact). These headings are both general (ID, Title etc.) and specific (Priority, Problem, department etc.) in manner and help in gaining summarized information about a submitted Suggestion.
