Useful Facts!

As an instructor, an evaluation nested inside the course will help you test how well an enrolled user has learned the course material.

This evaluation is available in the form of quizzes that can be attached or integrated into the course as course content that needs to be fulfilled to gain a certification!

Let’s see how a quiz is created!

Check out the Video Guide for a step-by-step walk through and detailed insights.

Step 1

Log into your Lean Suite Account with your credentials.

Step 2

Navigate to the Instructor Panel in LMS system.

https://app.intercom.com/a/apps/lhkbxbyv/knowledge-hub/internal-article/4673022

Step 3

Navigate to Your Learning Hub and click on View All Quizzes on the extreme right.

Useful Facts!

Make sure the Quizes tab is selected between the Courses & Quizzes buttons in the Learning Hub.

The All Quizzes dashboard will open up.

Click on the Add New button on the top right of the window.

Step 4

A Create Quiz window will open up.

Following are the major information fields while creating a Quiz:

Quiz Title

Enter the Quiz title.

Quiz Description

Enter the description of the quiz

Time Limit

This is a checkbox that allows you to make a quiz time bound or not.

Check the box as peer your preference for the quiz!

Useful Facts!

The time will be available to enter while you curate the quiz in the next step!

Just a reminder; the Create Quiz window takes in the basic information about a quiz to place it into the database and the further quiz is curated in another window!

Fill in all the information as required and proceed to click on Save on the top right corner.

A new quiz with the details entered will be created and green colored notification will show up confirming the creation.

This concludes the Part 1 - Adding a new Quiz. Go to the end of the manual to proceed to the second part!

Congratulations! We have successfully learned about the first part in the creating quiz process.

Now that we have added our quiz in the database, let’s move ahead and learn the quiz curation process!
