A Best Practice is a validated method or procedure recognized as the most effective way of performing a specific task or achieving a desired outcome. It represents the culmination of successful experimentation, learning, and standardization within an organization.

In summary, Best Practices in Lean Suite are the result of successful Kaizen initiatives that have been rigorously tested, refined, and standardized to optimize processes and drive continuous improvement. They serve as the foundation for excellence within the organization, guiding individuals in their daily work and enabling the achievement of operational goals.

TKP’s(Tactical Key Points)

By promoting the adoption of Best Practices, Lean Suite facilitates ongoing improvement and helps organizations adapt to changing circumstances in a dynamic business environment.
