Step 1

Log into your Lean Suite account with your credentials.

Need help? https://app.intercom.com/a/apps/lhkbxbyv/knowledge-hub/internal-article/4672769

Step 2

Navigate to the top right corner of the window and click on Create New.

Step 3

Select Team from the drop-down menu.

Step 4

A new Create Team window will show up with various fields to collect information about the new Team.

There are three stages in the Team Creation process and each one has different information fields:

Step 4.1 | Add Basic Info

Step 4.2 | Select Tools

Step 4.3 | Team Members

Step 5

After entering information in all three stages as required, click on Save on the bottom right corner to finish the process of creating a Team.

Congratulations, we have successfully learned how to create a Team in Skill Matrix!
