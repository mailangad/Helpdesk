Step 4

With the Team Charts tab selected, navigate to the All Tools sub-section and click on the pdf icon in the right corner.

A Save As dialogue box will appear as per your browser preferences allowing you to download the Team Data.

The Data Exported from the Team Charts section is in the form of a pdf and has radar chart details.
