LMS (Learning Management System)as the name describes is a tool inside Lean Suite that facilitates the flow of necessary knowledge inside a workspace using courses and quizzes on various topics.

The learning management system allows a streamlined way of administering crucial information along with an efficient way of testing the capability of the the learner.

The LMS can be used in many ways like employee orientation, upgrading skills, safety procedures etc. The capability of the LMS depends upon the company using it and when administered in a creative way can result in a holistic information and knowledge system that benefits the company and employees/workers simultaneously!
