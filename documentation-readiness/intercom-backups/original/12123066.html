As we know that the suggestion system aims to creates a holistic work environment where improvement ideas are welcomed as suggestions and fruitful initiatives are rewarded; the suggestion settings/preferences option contains options for the rewards aspect of suggestions.

Every time a user submits a suggestion, or a suggestion gets implemented, a fixed number of points are added to the user’s account enabling him to use them towards gaining rewards.

The options inside settings/preferences enables the admin to specify the number of points a user will gain for activities relating to suggestion system.

Follow the steps below to specify the number of points for activities:

Step 1

Navigate to the Suggestion System module by clicking the Suggestion button on the home page.

Step 2

Now click on Suggestion Settings from the left parent menu.

A new Preferences window will open up with a flow chart depicting a typical suggestion workflow.

The points awarded to any person involved are based on the steps in the flow chart right from submitting a suggestion to being implemented in form of a suggestion or a kaizen!

Step 3

Go to the top right corner of the window and click on Edit.

A new window will open up with various fields to input data.

There are two main inputs; one is the number of points on the right side and the other is a checkbox on the left.

The number of points simply means the rewards an employee would gaina t certain steps of the workflow. The checkbox button allows you to remove the option of awarding any points at a particular step.

When a field is ticked off it simply disappears from the flow chart.

Once you are done editing click on Save & Apply in the bottom right corner.

Congratulations! You have successfully learned about the preferences in suggestion module!
