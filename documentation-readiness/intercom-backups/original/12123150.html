Once a Suggestion is created, the module gives various options of attaching information to a Suggestion which is helpful for the successful understanding of the Suggestion. This helps to look at the situation in a more prospective way towards solving a reported problem/issue.

Step 1

Open the Suggestion you wish to attach information or data to.

Step 2

You can add information to a Suggestion in 3 different ways. Choose from below to learn further!

🔖Attach

Attach a file or document.

➰Sketch

Add an explanatory sketch.

🖼️Photo

Add a photo by using device camera
