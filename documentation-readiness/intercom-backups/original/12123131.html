As we know the activity section offers an interactive section where users/employees can comment and interact as a Suggestion goes through implementation; let’s learn about how to comment on a Suggestion!

Step 1

Open the Suggestion you wish to add a comment to in the side window or detail view as per your preference.

Need Help?

Step 2

Navigate to the Activity Section of the Suggestion.

Step 3

Choose Comment from the Show window to display the comment section.

Type in the comment in the Add a Comment box that shows up and click on the arrow on the extreme right of the box.

Step 4

After you’re finished check and confirm the submission of your comment as it will appear in the list below the comment box.

Congratulations! We have successfully learned how to comment on a Suggestion!
