Step 3

Click on View Details to open the Instructor Dashboard.

Step 4

Choose the instructor you wish to Edit or Delete.

Navigate to the extreme right of the row and click on the 3 dots under Action column.

Two options will show up Edit & Delete.

Edit

Click on edit and the Add Instructor window will show up.

After making required changes click on Update on the top right corner.

Delete

A browser pop will confirm whether you wish to delete the instructor from LMS.

Click on OK or Cancel as per your preference.

Congratulations! We have successfully learned how to edit and delete an instructor from LMS!
