Step 1

Navigate to the Instructor Panel or All Courses Dashboard and find the course you wish to add your quiz to.

Need Help?

https://app.intercom.com/a/apps/lhkbxbyv/knowledge-hub/internal-article/4673022

Step 2

Click on the course and it will open up the Course Creation window.

Step 3

Go to the Content Phase (Phase 4).

Choose Add Quiz from the two options available in the Course Content Field.

Step 4

A new field Select Quiz with a drop-down menu will show up.

Click anywhere in the light gray bar to bring out the quiz options.

The drop-down menu will show all the quizzes in the database.

Useful Facts!

The Quiz needs to be Published to be available in the options for drop-down menu. Quizzes in drafts are not available to attach to a course.

Select and click on the quiz you wish to add to your course, and it will be added to the course with additional options becoming visible.

Step 5

The additional options have four main options which are self-explanatory in nature:

  • Passing Marks

  • Do not grade this quiz

  • Pass the user in the entire course if this quiz is passed

  • Exclude the score if the quiz is failed

Just in case you wish to learn in detail about these or other options here’s a quick link!

https://theleansuite.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/KC/pages/edit-v2/187236384?draftShareId=3ea84ef4-e0f5-416b-b035-c43bf35dfbab

Enter the information in the additional options as required and click on Add when finished!
