Step 4

The Your Tasks dashboard will open up.

Tips & Tricks

By default, the Your Tasks dashboard opens up with the filter criteria Pending applied.

This means that upon opening the your tasks it automatically shows th instructor the pending ones.

To view other quizzes, change the Pending filter to passed or failed or clear the filter to view all quizzes.
