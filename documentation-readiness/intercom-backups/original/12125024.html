Step 3

Open the Tag Settings from Parent Menu and navigate to the Default Assignee option from the options available.

Step 4

Navigate to the top right corner and click on Create New.

A new Create Default Assignee window will open up.

Step 5

Enter the information as required and click on Save when finished.

The new assignee will appear in the list at the end.
