When a F-Tag is created, it collects information through various fields as per requirement.

This information is stored in all F-Tags, available to see through the dashboard.

After a F-Tag is submitted into the database, the information entered can be edited according to need as a F-Tag proceeds further.

There are two scope in which the information can be edited:

Properties Information

Information entered while creating a new F-Tag.

This scope covers the integral information that forms a F-Tag, ex. problem, recommendation, pillar, department, labels etc.
