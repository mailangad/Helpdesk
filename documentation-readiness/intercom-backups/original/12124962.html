Step 1

Log into your Lean Suite Account with you credentials.

Need Help?

https://app.intercom.com/a/apps/lhkbxbyv/knowledge-hub/internal-article/4672769

Step 2

Open the Best Practice you wish to share in Detail View.

Need Help?

https://app.intercom.com/a/apps/lhkbxbyv/knowledge-hub/internal-article/4673092

Useful Facts!

The Best Practice can be shared through detail view only.

Step 3

Navigate to the top bar options in detail view located below the title of the window.

Click on Share option located besides the Advance To button.

Step 4

A new Select Facilites window will open up.

Open the drop-down menu using the arrow and select the facilites you wish to share the Best Practice with.

Tips & Tricks

You can select more than one facility at a time. Just click from the options and the facilites are added to the field above.

Use the cross sign on the left of the facility name to remove a facility!

Once you have selected the facilites, click on Share.

Congratulations! We have successfully learned how to share a best practice with other facilities!

There might be other facilites that share a best practice with your facility too! When so, you can adopt or reject the best practice based on your requirements!

Let’s go ahead and learn how to adopt/reject a best practice shared with your facility!
