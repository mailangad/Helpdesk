Step 2

Once you reach the Savings Distribution dashboard, it might be toggled off in case it has not been activated previously.

Click on the toggle button to turn on the Savings Distribution.

Once the button is turned on further options will show up.

Step 3

The further options let’s a user to assign distribution percentages to the Lead, Coach, Sponsor & Team Members.

If a user wishes to equally distribute the savings, there is a separate button that automatically assigns equal values i.e. 25%.

Custom Values Entered

Step 4

Click on submit once you have entered the preferred values.
