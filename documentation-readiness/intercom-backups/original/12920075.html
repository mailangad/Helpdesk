Step 1

Navigate to the KPI dashboard from the home screen by clicking on the KPI button.

Step 2

Click on the KPI you wish to edit.

A new overlay window will open up showing the details of the KPI.

Step 3

Navigate to the top right corner of the overlay window and click on the pencil icon located left to the cross icon.

This is the edit button which takes you to the Create KPI window.

Here you can make changes to different sections as required.

Click on Publish once you are done to make the changes live.

Congratulations! We have successfully covered how to edit an existing KPI!
