Step 1

Log into your Lean Suite Account with your credentials.

Need Help?

https://app.intercom.com/a/apps/lhkbxbyv/knowledge-hub/internal-article/4672769

Step 2

Open the F-Tag Dashboard by clicking on the F-Tag Button on home page.

Step 3

Open the Tag Settings from Parent Menu and navigate to the Default Assignee option from the options available.

Step 4

Choose the Default Assignee yo wish to edit Navigate to the end of the row.

Click on the green pencil under the Action column.

A new Update Default Assignee window will open up.

Step 5

Enter the information as required and click on Save when finished.

The changes will take place and the default assignee will be updated with the new information.

Tips & Tricks

There is no pop-up notification for the update assignee therefore open the Update Default Assignee window again to make sure changes are in place!

Congratulations! We have successfully learned how to add a new Default Assignee in F-Tag Module!
