Step 1

Open the Lean Creator Dashboard by clicking on the Lean Creator Dashboard button on the home page.

Step 2

Choose the template you wish to edit and navigate to the last Action column.

Click on the three dots and a drop-down menu with two options will show up.

Step 3

Click on Edit to make changes to the template properties.

You can delete a template using the delete option in the window.

Once you click on edit, an Update Template window will open up.

Step 4

Make the required changes and click on Update located in the top right corner to save the changes!

Once you click on update, the template will open in the Lean Creator Window allowing you to make changes to the questions and responses if required.

You can either make the changes or simple press back and exit to the dashboard.

Congratulations! We have learned how to edit properties of a template!
