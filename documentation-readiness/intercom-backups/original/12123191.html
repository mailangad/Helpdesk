A campaign runs for the time range that was specified during the time of creation. A user can go to the campaign and see how it is performing based on the number of suggestions that are being submitted to the campaign.

Let’s have a look at how to see the detailed information of a campaign!

Step 1

Log into you Lean Suite Account.

Need Help?

Step 2

Go to the Suggestion Campaign Dashboard by clicking on the Campaigns button in the parent menu on the home page.

Step 3

The suggestion campaign dashboard displays information about campaigns through a Campaign Cards.

The Campaign Card is essentially similar to the side window of a kaizen, or suggestion that shows the information in a summarized manner.

It contains the cover image in the top, the submitting and editing options on the bottom right and the information on the bottom left side.

Campaign Card

A Campaign card will tell you about all the information about a campaign like date range, ID, name, number of points (Top right of the cover) and the number of submitted suggestions except the details of the submitted suggestions.

The details can be viewed in the Campaign Detail View.

To go to the campaign detail view, click anywhere in the campaign card.

A new Campaign window will open up with the details on the left and the submitted suggestions on the right side.

Suggestion Campaign Detail Window

A Campaign can be monitored and analyzed with this information!

Congratulations! We have successfully learned how to see campaign details and submitted suggestions!
