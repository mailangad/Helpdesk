Step 1

Log into your Lean Suite account with your credentials.

Step 2

Navigate to the Team Dashboard by clicking on the Skil Matrix button :Skill_Matrix_System: on the home page.

Step 3

Choose and click on the team you wish to see the data for a particular date.

The Team Detail window will open up.

Navigate to the top right corner and click on the calender icon inside the Select Date Box.

The Calender window will open up allowing you to choose the date to see the date for.

Useful Facts!

The Skill Matrix analyzes data for a particular year. The competency target is set by higher authorities during the start of the year preparations and the employees have the whole year meet the expected competency.

At any time between the year, the skill matric can show the progress of an employee's gap closure process!

Step 4

Once you select the date, the team detail window will update with the team statistical data until the selected date!

Tips & Tricks

By clicking on the excel icon right next to the date box, you can export the team results for that particular date!

Congratulations! We have successfully learned how to view team data for a particular date in Skill Matrix!
